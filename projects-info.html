New Development Sales

A Project Sales
Team That Thinks
Like Developers

Selling a new development is nothing like selling an existing home. It requires a deep understanding of product positioning, buyer psychology, staged release strategies, and the ability to build momentum before a single brick is laid.

We've spent years working alongside developers in the Illawarra — from boutique 4-lot townhouse projects through to 200-unit apartment stages — and we bring a commercial mindset to every engagement. Our goal is simple: maximise your revenue, minimise your time on market.

What We Deliver

End-To-End Project
Sales Services

01
Product Positioning

Before launch, we work with your team on pricing strategy, product mix, and naming. Getting this right sets up the entire campaign — a mispriced product wastes momentum you can never recover.

02
Display Suite & Marketing

We work with your marketing team to brief and review all campaign collateral — from renders and brochures through to digital channels and VIP launch events. Premium projects deserve premium presentation.

03
Release Strategy

Staged releases, FOMO pricing tiers, VIP allocations — we design your release strategy to build urgency and protect your margins across every stage of the campaign.

04
Buyer Registration & Qualification

We actively build and qualify a buyer database ahead of each launch — ensuring launch events are attended by genuine, ready purchasers, not tyre-kickers.
