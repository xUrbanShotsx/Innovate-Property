What We Do

The Illawarra's Development Specialists

Innovate Property Group has deep roots in the Illawarra development market. Whether you're a boutique builder seeking your next dual-occupancy block, a volume developer acquiring townhouse sites, or an investor looking for a large-scale apartment opportunity — we understand what makes a development site perform.

Our team works across the full spectrum of residential development land, from compact infill sites in established suburbs to large greenfield parcels on the city fringe. We bring buyers and sellers together with the market knowledge and negotiation expertise to make transactions happen efficiently.

Our Process

How We Work With Developers

01
Understand Your Brief

We take the time to understand your acquisition criteria — zoning, size, yield expectations, geography, and timeline — so we can source sites that genuinely fit.

02
Source & Identify

We leverage our local vendor relationships and market intelligence to identify development opportunities — including off-market sites that aren't publicly listed.

Our negotiation experience across development land means you'll be well-represented through price, terms, and conditions — protecting your position at every stage.
