The Prestige Market

What Makes A Prestige Property

Prestige property is defined not just by price, but by a combination of location, quality of construction, views, privacy, land size, and the lifestyle it affords. In the Illawarra, prestige homes often sit on elevated escarpment positions overlooking the Pacific, or front directly onto beaches and reserves.

At Innovate Property Group, we specialise exclusively in this segment — understanding that buyers at this level expect discretion, deep local knowledge, and an agent who can articulate the true value of exceptional property.

Key Locations

Where The Illawarra's Finest Homes Are Found

Barrack Point

Waterfront and near-water homes on the northern tip of Lake Illawarra — one of the region's most tightly held and sought-after residential addresses.

Shellharbour & Shell Cove

A master-planned coastal village with marina frontage, modern prestige homes, and a lifestyle that rivals anything the NSW coast has to offer.

Kiama & Kiama Downs

Clifftop and elevated properties with dramatic Pacific Ocean views, large land parcels, and a heritage seaside village atmosphere.

Wollongong Escarpment

Properties perched on the Illawarra Escarpment with sweeping coastal panoramas — unique in NSW for combining mountain bushland with ocean views.

Flinders & Surrounds

Sought-after acreage and lifestyle properties south of Shellharbour — spacious, private, and increasingly popular with buyers seeking room to breathe.
