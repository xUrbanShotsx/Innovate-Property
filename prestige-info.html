The Prestige Market

The Illawarra's
Upper End,
Redefined

The Illawarra prestige market is unlike anywhere else in Australia. Nestled between the escarpment and the Pacific, properties in the $1.5M–$10M+ bracket offer a lifestyle that urban markets simply cannot replicate — and a level of scarcity that consistently drives long-term capital growth.

Browse Listings

We operate exclusively in this market segment — not as a side offering, but as a core discipline. Our team understands the nuance of pricing a prestige home, the discretion required in the sales process, and the calibre of buyers you want walking through the door.

Every prestige listing we take on receives a tailored marketing strategy, professional styling consultation, architectural photography, and access to our curated database of qualified buyers — many of whom never appear on the open market.

Whether you're buying your forever home or making a strategic acquisition, Innovate Property Group brings the expertise, network, and dedication your property deserves.

Key Locations

Where Prestige
Meets Place

Austinmer
Clifftop & Oceanfront

One of the most tightly held coastal villages in the region. Clifftop homes with direct Pacific views consistently set suburb records.

Kiama
Coastal Township

Historic coastal charm with strong appeal to lifestyle buyers relocating from Sydney. The Kiama peninsula commands a significant prestige premium.

Bulli
Village Lifestyle

A village that punches well above its weight — prestige buyers drawn to the escarpment backdrop, surf access, and tight-knit community.

Thirroul
D.H. Lawrence Country

Creative heritage, oceanfront access, and an increasingly gentrified main street make Thirroul one of the Illawarra's most desirable prestige addresses.

Gerringong
Southern Escarpment

Rolling farmland meets dramatic Pacific views. Acreage properties and luxury coastal estates draw buyers from across the country.

Wollongong Harbour
City Waterfront

Boutique luxury apartments and architecturally designed homes overlooking Belmore Basin — Wollongong's most prestigious city-fringe address.
