Our Process

How We Sell
Prestige Homes

Step 01
Private Consultation
We begin with a confidential meeting at your property. We listen to your goals, timeline, and expectations before offering any advice — because the right strategy starts with the right understanding.
Step 02
Market Appraisal & Strategy
We provide a detailed, data-backed appraisal of your property and present a tailored sales strategy — including method of sale, pricing guidance, campaign timing, and target buyer profile.
Step 03
Presentation & Styling
First impressions drive premium prices. We coordinate professional photography, videography, and styling where appropriate, ensuring your property is presented at its absolute best across all platforms.
Step 04
Campaign Launch
We launch your campaign across our full digital and network channels — including targeted digital advertising, database marketing to qualified buyers, and exclusive pre-market exposure to our private buyer list.
Step 05
Buyer Qualification
Every enquiry is personally handled by our team. We qualify buyers, arrange private inspections, and maintain constant communication — ensuring only serious, capable buyers proceed to the negotiation stage.
Step 06
Negotiation & Exchange
We negotiate with precision on your behalf, leveraging competitive interest and deep market knowledge to achieve the strongest possible outcome. We manage the full negotiation through to exchange of contracts.
Step 07
Settlement & Beyond
Our service doesn't end at exchange. We coordinate with solicitors, manage the settlement process, and remain available to you throughout — and beyond — ensuring a seamless experience from listing to handover.
